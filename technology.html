Engineering & development

My background is in chemical engineering, TCP/IP networking, network security and voice systems.

Learning to build again

I’m doing development again for the first time in 30 years. I have some Unix background and a lot of experience with networks and software businesses, but I’m relearning programming across languages and frameworks. I don’t have formal training in algorithms, data structures or software engineering.

AI is part of what makes this interesting. I can spend more time building and testing ideas, while learning what the tools are actually doing. I still need to understand enough to judge the result. GitHub is where you can find my public work.

The internet years

I moved into computer consulting and internet infrastructure in Japan in the mid-1990s. At PSINet, I had nine different roles over about four years as the business grew. The work ranged from engineering and customer support to running parts of the business.

Telephony made the connection between engineering and customer expectations particularly clear. A service could make sense technically and still be missing something customers considered essential, such as connecting a fax machine.
