JIM WEISSER / POLICY

Policy & public work

Much of my work outside individual companies has been through the American Chamber of Commerce in Japan.

The internet economy

I was heavily involved in the ACCJ’s Internet Economy Task Force and in preparing and releasing the internet economy documents. That work brought business experience into discussions about how Japan and the United States could benefit from a growing internet economy.

I was in the embassy when we agreed to get the dialogue started as something that would benefit both countries. I worked with Eriko Asai, Jim Foster of Microsoft and others across business and policy.

ACCJ Internet Economy White Paper · Japan’s Ministry of Foreign Affairs, October 2010

ACCJ leadership

I have served as an ACCJ governor and vice president. I was on the board when the chamber received the U.S. Department of Commerce’s Peace Through Commerce medal, announced in 2013 for 2012.

Working in a volunteer organization also taught me something I needed as a manager: people have to want to work with you. A title alone does not get the work done.
