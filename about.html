A little more about me

Fort Worth, Rice, Japan, family, sport, wine, and questions about how we learn.

Outside the office

I grew up in Fort Worth, Texas, and studied chemical engineering at Rice University. I've lived in Japan since 1993. I follow the NFL and UFC, enjoy wine, and played American football in Japan for three years.

True fact about Rice when I was there: we received sheepskin diplomas. Truly old school. When some students objected, they were given paper diplomas. I'm always happy when a noisy minority gets its own choice without dictating the choice for the majority.

I care about education reform and AI. I'm a big fan of The Innovation Fellowship in Shibuya, where students work on projects they care about. I'm skeptical of how much time education can spend getting people to follow instructions instead of helping them learn. Returning to development gives me plenty of opportunities to be a beginner again. I love making new mistakes.

Time and family

I enjoy my work, but consciously prioritize how my work and life fit together. For any competent person, there is an infinite amount of work to do, so I try to be deliberate about how I spend my time, including time with my children.

I talked about this, and what parenting taught me about management, in this interview with Wahl+Case.
