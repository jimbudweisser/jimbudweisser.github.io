A little more about me

Texas, Japan, family, sport, wine, and questions about how we learn.

Outside the office

I grew up in Texas and have lived in Japan since 1993. I follow the NFL and UFC, enjoy wine, and played American football in Japan for three years.

I care about education reform and AI. I believe in learning, but I’m skeptical of how much time education can spend getting people to follow instructions instead of helping them learn. Returning to development gives me plenty of opportunities to be a beginner again.

Time and family

I get a lot of satisfaction from work, but I also want to spend time with my children. There is always more work to do. I have to make a conscious decision about what gets my time.

I talked about this, and what parenting taught me about management, in this interview with Wahl+Case.
